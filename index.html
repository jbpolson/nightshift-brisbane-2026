The Event

Racing under lights.

Nightshift takes over Murarrie for one night only. Fast racing, loud corners, food, drinks, music, and the kind of trackside energy usually missing from local crits.

On Saturday, 20 June, we're turning Brisbane's home of criterium racing into an after-dark race night for riders, spectators, friends, families, and anyone who thinks cycling is better when it gets a little less sensible.

The Venue

Murarrie Criterium Track

Brisbane's purpose-built closed-circuit home for fast racing. No road closures. Purpose-built for criteriums. Strong spectator visibility. Space for the full event village.

The challenge is making Murarrie feel different. The site gets dressed, lit, and activated — so it doesn't just feel like a regular crit night with a banner on the fence.
